03 / THE AMBIGUITY WITNESS
Same local evidence.
Different reality.
One worker dies before sending. Another dies after the destination commits. Both leave the same durable local intent record: the result is unknown.
After the retention horizon, the guarded policy stops in both histories. One has already applied the effect. The other has not.

04 / METHOD & BOUNDARIES
What is original here?
The integrated benchmark, evidence package, independent metric verifier and paired witness. Keys, receipts, payload binding and quarantine are established techniques.
Read the prior-work assessment ↗
What was measured?
Effect counts, payload consistency, false confirmations, explicit stops and database integrity. Four policies, eight schedules, twelve input seeds and three synthetic labels.
Read the complete protocol ↗
What remains open?
Authenticated destination reconciliation, concurrent workers, realistic clock and network failures, provider restarts and external replication. No production or universal exactly-once claim.
